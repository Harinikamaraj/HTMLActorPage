Vijay

Vijay was born on 22 June 1974 in Madras (now Chennai), Tamil Nadu, to film director S. A. Chandrasekhar and playback singer Shoba Chandrasekhar. He had a younger sister, Vidhya, who passed away at age two. Vijay attended Fathima Matriculation Higher Secondary School and Balalok Matriculation School, later enrolling in Loyola College for a degree in Visual Communications, which he dropped to pursue acting.
